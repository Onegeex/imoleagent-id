How it works

Agentic Wallets can't connect to websites, so there is no "connect wallet" here. Your own AI agent does the paying, and you confirm every transaction in the Binance app.

01

Ask your agent

"Top up mama.ai with 1 USDT." The Imole skill resolves the name to a meter, quotes the kWh and checks your limits.

02

You confirm

It builds an exact-amount approval and the top-up, shows the official baw preview, and waits for your yes.

03

The light comes on

The meter sees new credit and closes its relay. It signs its own usage reports, and the installer is paid only for energy actually used.

Give your agent the skill

Works with Binance AI Pro, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and any agent that has the Binance Agentic Wallet skill. You need an Agent ID and Developer Mode on (Agentic Wallet → Settings).

npx skills add binance/binance-skills-hub --skill binance-agentic-wallet
npx skills add Onegeex/imoleagent-id --skill imole-energy

Using the imole-energy skill, sponsor onegeex.ai's Imole meter with 0.2 USDT from my Agentic Wallet. Show me the preview and wait for my confirmation.
Using the imole-energy skill, never let top-ups to onegeex.ai's meter exceed 2 USDT each or 5 USDT per week.

Safety, by design

The contract is open source and verified. Here is exactly what it can and can't do.

  • No owner, admin, pause, upgrade or mint function exists in ImoleGrid
  • Escrowed USDT moves only against usage signed by the meter's own key
  • Only Agent ID holders can top up, sponsor or set limits
  • Exact-amount approvals only, never unlimited
  • Your own spending limits on-chain; raising one waits 24 hours
  • No QR-code or pairing-code login, no wallet sessions stored by this site
  • This site only reads public chain data; it never asks for keys
  • .ai names resolve only to a meter for payment, never to balances or history
Risk notice. Energy credit is a prepayment for electricity on a specific meter and is not refundable or transferable. Sponsoring is a gift with no financial return. Crypto transactions are irreversible; check every preview before you confirm. Imole is not affiliated with or endorsed by Binance.
